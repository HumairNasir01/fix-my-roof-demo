Specialists in Single-Story Concrete Tile Roofs in North Brisbane

At FIX MY ROOF Total Roof Restorations, Dino and the team specialize exclusively in single-story concrete tile roofs across North Brisbane, QLD. By dedicating 100% of our focus to this specific roof profile, we have refined every aspect of roof restoration—from safety procedures and precision cleaning to proprietary bonding primers and membrane application.

We believe homeowners deserve a tradesman who treats their home with absolute care and respect. That is why Dino prioritizes customer satisfaction above all else. When you contact FIX MY ROOF, you deal directly with Dino from your initial roof inspection and written quote through to the final coat of paint. There are no commission-hungry salesmen, no junior subcontractors, and no unexpected surprises.

Our comprehensive 5-step approach ensures a long-lasting and aesthetically pleasing result. By using genuine, Australian-manufactured Industrial Roof Coatings membranes, we ensure every concrete tile roof we restore can withstand Queensland’s blistering summer heat, relentless storms, and persistent humidity.
