Welcome to FIX MY ROOF

North Brisbane’s Trusted Concrete Tile Restoration Specialist

Welcome to FIX MY ROOF Total Roof Restorations. Owned and operated by Dino, we take immense pride in delivering top-tier craftsmanship and remarkable transformations for single-story concrete tile roofs throughout Bridgeman Downs and North Brisbane.

Unlike large corporate roofing franchises that dispatch unfamiliar subcontractors, Dino personally assesses, quotes, and restores your roof. Using a proven 5-step methodology—combining commercial rotary pressure washing, full ridge cap repointing, tile replacements, penetrating primer, and two thick coats of Industrial Roof Coatings membrane—we ensure your home looks stunning and stays leak-free for years to come.
