Our Proven 5-Step Restoration Process

Dino specialises exclusively in single-story concrete tile roofs in North Brisbane. Every restoration is executed using this meticulous 5-step method to ensure exceptional longevity and aesthetic appeal.

Step 01

Pressure Washing

Deep cleaning of the concrete roof tiles. Utilizing high-pressure commercial rotary washers up to 4000 PSI to thoroughly eliminate decades of embedded dirt, black mold, moss, and lichen down to bare concrete.

  • Removes 100% of moss, algae, and airborne pollutants
  • Complete gutter cleaning and flush included
  • Thorough ground, wall, and garden rinse down
Step 02

Repointing

Secure and durable repointing of ridge capping. We remove loose, broken mortar and apply a premium flexible polymer pointing compound that bonds securely and flexes naturally with Brisbane’s temperature swings.

  • Eliminates loose, dislodged ridge capping caps
  • Flexible compound engineered for extreme thermal movement
  • Crisp, laser-straight troweled architectural finish
Step 03

Tile Replacement

Safe removal and replacement of any broken roof tiles. Dino physically inspects every tile on your roof, discarding any tiles with hairline fractures, cracks, or corner chips and replacing them with exact-match profiles.

  • Guaranteed profile and contour matching for concrete tiles
  • Prevents concealed leaks and batten water rot
  • Spare coated tiles left behind for the homeowner
Step 04

Primer Coat

Application of a high-quality bonding PRIMER coat. Concrete tiles naturally become chalky and porous over time. This specially formulated penetrating primer seals porous surfaces, creating an unbreakable substrate lock.

  • Deep concrete pore penetration for maximum bonding strength
  • Prevents premature flaking, peeling, and blistering
  • Ensures even topcoat colour saturation and finish
Step 05 • Final Protection

Industrial Membrane Coating

Application of 2 heavy-duty coats of INDUSTRIAL ROOF COATINGS membrane. Made in Australia specifically for our intense UV index and violent storm downpours, this thick elastomeric barrier shields your roof and creates a deep, glossy, showroom-quality aesthetic.

  • Two full coats of genuine Industrial Roof Coatings membrane
  • Exceptional UV resistance, dirt pick-up resistance, and weatherproofing
  • Available in popular Brisbane colors (Monument, Charcoal, Terracotta & more)
